AI-enabled Cyber Forensics Lab

School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, University of Queensland

Advancing AI-driven detection and forensic analysis of manipulated media through multimodal learning, privacy-preserving techniques, and explainable frameworks for real-world impact.

About the Group

Led by Dr. Priyanka Singh, a Senior Lecturer in Cyber Security, our research group focuses on tackling the critical challenges posed by deepfakes, synthetic media, and multimodal misinformation. With a PhD in Image Forensics and postdoctoral experience at prestigious institutions (SUNY Albany, Dartmouth College), Dr. Singh brings expertise in digital forensics, cybersecurity, privacy-preserving techniques, and AI-driven content authentication.

We work at the intersection of computer vision, multimedia forensics, and AI security, developing robust detection frameworks and evaluation benchmarks that serve high-stakes applications in elections, healthcare, law enforcement, and platform integrity.

Collaborations: We actively collaborate with government agencies, media integrity organizations, industry partners, and international research communities to advance the state-of-the-art in misinformation detection.

Research Areas

🖼️ Multimodal Misinformation

Detection and analysis of falsified or staged visual content combined with misleading captions, addressing narrative-level inconsistencies beyond pixel-level artifacts.

🎭 Deepfake Detection & Attribution

Audio, visual, and video forensic techniques to identify synthetic media generation methods and trace manipulation origins with explainability for non-expert users.

🔐 Privacy-Preserving Forensics

Secure approaches using homomorphic encryption and secret sharing for authentication and tampering detection without exposing sensitive data.

⚔️ AI Safety & Adversarial Robustness

Mitigation of AI-based attacks including adversarial perturbations, jailbreak prompts, and coordinated abuse in vision-language systems.

💡 Explainable AI for Media Verification

Interactive and human-interpretable frameworks enabling non-expert users to understand deepfake detection decisions and verify content authenticity.

🛡️ Cyber Threat Intelligence Sharing

Secure and collaborative approaches to CTI data exchange with robust privacy protection for AI-driven cybersecurity incident response.
